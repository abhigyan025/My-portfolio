Meet Kumar Abhigyan

Kumar Abhigyan is an emerging writer and independent thinker focused on clarity, systems, and long-term understanding...

Kumar Abhigyan is an emerging writer and independent thinker focused on clarity, systems, and long-term understanding. Born in October 2011 and based in Jharkhand, he represents a generation that has grown up with constant information — but chooses to approach it with discipline rather than reaction.

His work is centered around making sense of complex realities. Instead of responding to events at face value, he approaches them as parts of larger systems — interconnected, layered, and evolving over time. This perspective reflects in both his books and essays, where the goal is not just to inform, but to help the reader see more clearly.

Beyond writing, Kumar actively engages in debate and structured discussion. His interest lies not in winning arguments, but in refining thought — identifying patterns, testing assumptions, and building understanding through reasoning. This approach has shaped the way he communicates ideas: calm, deliberate, and grounded in observation rather than noise.

He also maintains a consistent presence on platforms like Instagram, where he shares insights, updates, and simplified explanations of global and societal developments. This serves as an extension of his broader objective — to make complex ideas accessible without reducing their depth.

His areas of interest are diverse, yet connected through a common thread of curiosity and systems thinking. Motorsports reflects an interest in precision and performance. Cooking represents process and transformation. The study of human behaviour connects directly to his writing. Exploration, both intellectual and physical, remains constant.

At the core of his work is a consistent principle: clarity over noise. In a time where information is abundant but understanding is often shallow, his focus remains on slowing down, observing patterns, and building insights that hold over time.

US–Iran War — WW3?

The conflict between the United States and Iran is often misunderstood as a recent issue. In reality, it is built over decades...

Before 1979, Iran and the United States were closely aligned. However, events such as the 1953 coup created underlying distrust...

1979 changed everything. The revolution reshaped Iran’s identity and its perception of the United States...

Over time, nuclear tensions, regional conflicts, and competing interests layered onto this distrust...

It is not one reason. It is layers.

World War II — The Event That Shaped the Modern World

World War II was not just a war. It was a restructuring of global power...

Europe weakened. The United States and Soviet Union rose...

Institutions like the UN and NATO emerged...

The Cold War defined global politics for decades...

The world we see today is still shaped by those outcomes.

India — Rising Power or Structural Challenge?

India is often described as a rising power. But rise is not linear...

Growth exists, but structural challenges remain...

Demographics, technology, and geopolitics offer strength...

Execution and consistency will define the future...

The question is not rise. It is sustainability.
