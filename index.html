Meet Kumar Abhigyan

Kumar Abhigyan is a young Indian author and commentator exploring the modern world through analytical and reflective writing.

Kumar Abhigyan (born October 2010) is a young Indian author, writer, and commentator from Jharkhand, India. His work reflects a deep curiosity about global affairs, society, and power structures.Through his writing, he presents thoughtful, data-driven perspectives shaped by observation and critical thinking. He is also known for his achievements in multiple online debates focused on geopolitics. Beyond writing, he explores music, motorsports, cooking, and the dynamics of the world around him.
