Kumar Abhigyan (born October 2010) is a young Indian author, writer, and commentator from Jharkhand, India. He writes analytical, data-driven texts that reflect his perspective on the world, society, politics, economy, and culture, aiming to present the thoughts of an Indian teenager in a clear, respectful, and critical manner. Abhigyan developed a strong curiosity about global affairs, faith, and societal dynamics from an early age. His writing combines reflection with analysis, allowing readers to engage with complex ideas through the lens of a thoughtful young observer. Abhigyan is also known for his achievements in multiple online debates, particularly those focused on geopolitics, where he has won numerous competitions, consistently making his points clear and persuasive. Beyond his intellectual pursuits, he enjoys music, motorsports, cooking, exploring the world around him, and participating in discussions.
